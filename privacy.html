Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t collect any information about you on its own. This page explains the little that does happen, as the EU’s data protection rules (GDPR) require.

Who is responsible

Dante Persson, Malmö, Sweden. You can reach me at dantep@live.se about anything on this page.

What is collected

  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel’s servers briefly record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser and the time, to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks. This is based on a legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR article 6(1)(f)). Vercel may process this data outside the EU; how, and the safeguards it uses, are described in Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Email. If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don’t share them. They stay in my inbox for as long as the conversation is relevant.

What is not collected

  • No cookies and no cookie banner, because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking of any kind.
  • No outside services. Fonts and everything else load from this site itself, so visiting doesn’t send your details to Google or anyone else.
  • Light or dark mode. If you switch theme, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and TokenWise AI lead to other websites with their own privacy rules. Nothing is shared with them until you click.

Your rights

You have the right to ask what personal data I have about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it’s used. Email me and I’ll answer within a month. If you think your data has been handled wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
