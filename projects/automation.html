Track work automation

Excel tools with VBA macros that fill in the planning and safety documents for our track work, so nobody on the team has to write them by hand.

Role
Idea, planning and build
When
May 2026
Status
In daily use
Users
Team of 6, 5 projects

The problem

Every track work job needs planning and safety documents, and we wrote them by hand. It took hours every day. It’s safety-critical paperwork, so every manual step was also a chance to get something wrong.

What I built

I built Excel tools with VBA macros that generate the documents from the details of each job. I used AI tools to help me write the code.

Results

saved every day
700–1,400 h

~10 h

Fewer manual steps also means fewer human errors in safety-critical documents, and more time for the work on site.
