Why I built it

I wanted to explain AI to companies without hype or jargon, and with real numbers: what tokens are, what AI costs, and what it can save.

It was also a test for myself: can I use AI to build something of real value, from idea to a live site?

What’s on the site

  • How AI works, in four plain steps, plus what it’s good at and where to be careful.
  • Where the savings come from, with the formula shown openly and a realistic range instead of “100% automated”.
  • Tokens explained: a live counter where you paste text and see what it would cost on different models.
  • A savings estimate for your own task, and a chat that checks if a business problem is a good fit for AI.
  • A booking flow for consulting calls.

How I worked

The site leads visitors through three steps: understand how AI works, see what it costs, then check their own idea. I built it with AI coding tools and published it on Vercel.
