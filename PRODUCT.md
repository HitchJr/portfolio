# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: hiring managers and recruiters for railway, logistics, project and operations roles (including operations manager roles), in Sweden and abroad. They open the site from a CV or LinkedIn link, usually skim for under a minute, and decide whether to contact Dante or download a CV. Secondary: IT and tech employers looking at the AI and coding work.

## Product Purpose
A job-application portfolio for Dante Persson. It should make an operations hiring manager believe, within seconds, that Dante is a calm, competent railway operations lead who also builds useful tools, and then let them contact him or download the right CV. It should read as a portfolio, not as someone selling a service, and invite any kind of work (on-site, hybrid or remote).

## Positioning
A safety-critical railway operations professional (train traffic control and TLS programming at Trafikverket, now Chief Track Work Supervisor leading track-work projects) who plans and builds work tools and web apps with AI, and is honest about where his own skills end and the AI's begin.

## Operating Context
Swedish railway operations: Trafikverket's 24/7 traffic control centre, TLS (the traffic control system), TTJ traffic rules, track protection planning (A-, E-, L- and S-skydd), track-work sites, timetables and train graphs. Dante is from Trelleborg and based in Malmö.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step; deployed on Vercel from github.com/HitchJr/portfolio.
- Three CV PDFs live in cv/ (Standard, Project management, Operations and IT).
- Dante is a beginner coder: fixes VBA on his own; builds web apps by directing Claude Code. Never overstate his coding level.

## Brand Commitments
- Personal brand name DP.IO; the logo (lowercase "dp" with a rail signal head, and the lockup with "Dante Persson" over a route line in brand/) must be kept.
- Colours, typography and the rest of the visual language may change.
- The railway idea should stay, but the departure board may take another form.
- Plain wording in Dante's own voice.

## Evidence on Hand
- Work history and results from the CVs: 2022–2026 Train Traffic Controller and TLS Programmer at Trafikverket (operational TLS programmer within 6 months after joining the three-person programming team); May 2026–now Chief Track Work Supervisor at LD Järnväg & Planering AB (several teams, customer contact, project planning); Excel/VBA tools used daily by a team of 6 across 5 projects, saving 2–4 work hours a day and about 10 hours per documentation request; Postnord Salesforce superuser after one year.
- Projects: Workflow automation (internal), VoiceReader (private repo), TokenWise AI (live at tokenwise-ai.vercel.app), Rail Pulse (paused).
- No photos of Dante, no testimonials, no employer logos on hand. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Operations credibility first, tools second.
- Prove with real numbers from the CV; never invent claims.
- Honest about coding level.
- Calm and precise, like safety-critical work.
